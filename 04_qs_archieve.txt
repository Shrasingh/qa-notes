We introduced a hybrid persistence model where invoices are stored both in a fast in-memory cache for immediate PDF rendering and in a durable MySQL JSON store for long-term archival.

We enforce business rules such as editability through an explicit editable flag in the database rather than inferring it dynamically, allowing future role-based flexibility.

The slug is made unique to enforce idempotent updates.
If the slug remains the same, we overwrite the existing document; if it changes, a new record is created automatically due to unique constraint behavior.
